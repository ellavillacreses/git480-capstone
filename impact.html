The Impact Behind the Music

Festivals play a key role in both boosting and sustaining local economies within Arizona. They attract visitors from both close by and states away, increasing the overall amount of money flowing into these areas. This generates a higher and more consistent revenue for the businesses located around festivals. When a festival happens, the ticket price is not the only expense. Visitors will purchase tickets, food, drinks, transportation, and potentially hotels among other things. This increased spending benefits all different types of businesses located around the festival, with the most concentrated festival presence activity being located in Phoenix, Tucson, and Tempe.

Another positive impact that festivals bring is the creation of jobs and increased business opportunities. Festivals require a high volume of people for pre and post production, with assistance needed for setup, event production, security, transportation, and food and merchandising services. This creates multiple job openings that benefit local residents as well as the local community. Local vendors and businesses require a higher volume of workers around festival dates as well, due to increased customer traffic.

The long term economic benefit of festivals is also key, as tourism growth occurs in each town where festivals are established. With such a diverse set of genres, these festivals attract a higher volume of varying audiences. Increased tourism helps to generate additional revenue due to visitors spending more on transportation, shopping, food and beverage, and entertainment.
